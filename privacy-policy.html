Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 November 2026

Introduction

WLSC Consulting Pte. Ltd. ("WLSC Consulting", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of visitors to wlscconsulting.com as well as our business clients and partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal and corporate information in connection with this website and our B2B consulting interactions. Your use of this website is also governed by our Terms of Use.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: When you contact us by email, submit our contact form, or engage with us regarding consulting services, we collect the contact details, business requirements, and project-related information you choose to share (such as your name, work email address, company details, and message content).

Server, hosting, and technical data: When you visit this website, our web hosting and infrastructure providers automatically record standard technical and server log data (such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages requested, and access timestamps). This technical data is processed to deliver web pages reliably, maintain availability, and protect the site against abuse or security threats. We do not place behavioral analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal and corporate information solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to business inquiries and communicating with prospective and current clients.
  • Delivering consulting, software development, and data science services as agreed under separate written client agreements.
  • Managing project progress, deliverables, invoicing, and administrative records.
  • Operating, securing, and troubleshooting this website and preventing abuse.
  • Fulfilling contractual, regulatory, and legal obligations.

Data Sharing and Recipient Categories

WLSC Consulting Pte. Ltd. does not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We disclose personal data only where necessary to the following functional categories of recipients:

  • Web hosting, DNS, and infrastructure service providers that deliver this website and help maintain availability and security.
  • Contact form submission and business email delivery providers that route inquiries you send to us.
  • Professional advisers (such as legal, accounting, and audit advisers) bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulatory, law enforcement, or judicial authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law or lawful legal process.

Do Not Track and Third-Party Tracking

Because this website does not track visitors across third-party websites or operate behavioral analytics or advertising scripts, it does not alter its data collection practices in response to browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. We do not allow third parties to collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you visit this website.

Children's Privacy

This website and our consulting services are intended for business professionals and are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact info@wlscconsulting.com and we will promptly delete it.

Data Protection

WLSC Consulting Pte. Ltd. implements reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal and client information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. These measures include access controls, secure digital storage, and periodic review of our security practices.

Data Retention

We retain personal and client information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, support follow-up inquiries after an engagement, or comply with applicable legal, tax, and accounting requirements. Technical server logs are retained only for short operational and security windows maintained by our hosting infrastructure.

Your Rights and Appeals

Depending on applicable law (including Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable privacy laws), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held by WLSC Consulting Pte. Ltd., or withdraw consent where applicable, by emailing info@wlscconsulting.com. We will verify and respond to your request within the time required by applicable law.

If we decline your privacy request in whole or in part, you may appeal our decision by emailing info@wlscconsulting.com with the subject line "Privacy Request Appeal". We will review and respond to your appeal within the time required by applicable law, and you also retain the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to This Policy

WLSC Consulting Pte. Ltd. may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If we make material changes to how we collect or use personal data, we will post a prominent notice on this website prior to or upon the changes taking effect.
